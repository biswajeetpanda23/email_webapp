How the filter works

Selection priority (max 2 per row)

  • 1st — best personal email (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook…)
  • 2nd — best business email on matching domain only
  • Person-name emails preferred over role emails
  • Role order: CEO → Founder → Owner → Director → HR…
  • Business email domain must match row domain exactly

Blocked automatically

  • Domain column: entire row skipped if domain has .org .edu .gov .pk .jp .bd .lk .app .xyz
  • Multi-level domains: gwct.org.uk skipped via .org segment
  • Email keywords: noreply, test, fake, seo, ngo, press…
  • Platforms: google, livejournal, tripod, tripadvisor
  • Wrong-domain business emails (e.g. mspence@janklow.com for eugenelim.com)
